YKA Vault Manager 1.0.2

This build has not completed real YubiKey hardware or clean-computer acceptance. Keep independent backups of important data.

Setup installs YKA under Program Files. If WinFsp is missing, Setup installs the bundled, signed WinFsp 2.1 package. WinFsp is required to mount encrypted folders. A restart may be required, but Setup will not force one.

YKA normally runs without administrator privileges. Startup at sign-in is off by default and can be enabled in Settings.

Upgrading or uninstalling YKA does not remove your saved vault list, personal settings, encrypted folders or Keyrings. WinFsp is not removed because other applications may use it.

Before upgrading or uninstalling, use the YKA tray menu to unmount all vaults and exit. Never store personal vaults inside the application installation directory.
